Strike Clash – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Strike Clash handles information when you use the Strike Clash mobile game.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Strike Clash and its service providers may process information needed to operate the game, provide advertising, maintain security, and measure app performance. This may include device or advertising identifiers, basic device information, game activity, and information you provide directly.

Advertising

Strike Clash may display advertisements. Advertising providers may process information such as advertising identifiers and device information according to their own privacy policies and applicable choices available on your device.

How We Use Information

  • To operate and maintain the game.
  • To provide game features and prevent abuse or fraud.
  • To understand app performance and improve the game.
  • To show and measure advertisements where applicable.

Children

Strike Clash is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us.

Third-Party Services

Strike Clash may use third-party services for functions such as advertising, app distribution, analytics, or other technical services. Those providers may process information under their own policies.

Data Retention and Security

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above or as required by law. Reasonable measures are used to protect information, although no internet or electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your Choices

You may be able to reset or limit advertising identifiers and advertising personalization through your Android device settings. You may also contact us about privacy questions or deletion requests.
